Simple ringtones for everyone: big buttons, loud tones and your own voice.
---
Easy Ringtone makes it simple to change the sounds of your phone. It is made for older people and anyone who finds phone settings confusing.

Big buttons, large text and short words. Every tone can be heard before you choose it.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Call ringtone, message tone and alarm tone – all from one screen
• Use a song or sound from your phone, or any tone already on the phone
• Record your own voice, or a grandchild's voice, and use it as a ringtone
• WhatsApp, Messenger, imo and other apps: simple steps to give each app its own sound
• Volume and vibration: Sound, Vibrate or Silent, and big − and + buttons for every volume
• "Do Not Disturb" warning, so you know why the phone is quiet

LOUD TONES FOR WEAK HEARING
A separate section with extra-loud tones made for people who hear less. They use low, strong sounds that are easier to hear. One big red button makes the phone as loud as it can be.

ENGLISH AND URDU
The whole app works in English and in Urdu (اردو). Switch with one tap.

PRIVATE
No account and no sign-in. Your recordings and choices stay on your phone.

The app shows one small ad at the bottom of the home screen. There are no full-screen ads.
